# Kid tests: SPEC-012

**Purpose:** record whether children can say each rewritten lesson's big idea back (SC-002), and whether the 8–11 text feels too young (SC-004).

**How:**
- The owner runs each session with a parent present.
- There is no audio or video recording, and no names or other identifying details are written down.
- Each child reads or listens to the lesson in their own band, then says the big idea in their own words.

**A lesson passes when:**
- at least 4 of 5 children in **each** band say the big idea;
- the 8–11 group includes at least one child aged 10–11;
- no 10–11-year-old calls the 8–11 text "for little kids".
